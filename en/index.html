What leaves your PC – and what doesn’t

Many PDF programs run AI and text recognition in the cloud. GetVaultPDF does both on your computer. Here is exactly what data goes where.

Internet

Only when you trigger it

  • License activation, once: license key and a random device name
  • Downloads you start: installer, Ollama and the AI model

Never

  • Documents, file names or contents
  • Usage data or tracking
  • Cloud AI: even Ollama’s cloud models are blocked

Your own servers and devices, only if you set them up

  • AI server on your company network, local addresses only
  • Your own Paperless-ngx server
  • Scanners and printers

Your PC

  • Your documents and file names
  • Text recognition (OCR)
  • AI assistant via Ollama
  • Converting Word, Excel, emails and web pages
  • Redacting, signing, forms
  • License check after activation

Hidden instructions don’t get through

For emails and web pages, the AI only receives what is visible in the document. Invisible text, such as instructions disguised in an email, never reaches the model.

No tracking through remote images

When opening emails, GetVaultPDF doesn’t load any images from the internet. The sender won’t find out that you opened the message.

AI server on your own network only

If a PC on your company network runs the AI for everyone, GetVaultPDF only connects to addresses on the local network. It sends nothing to servers on the internet – not even when a VPN or proxy program disguises them as local addresses.

Frequently asked questions

Is GetVaultPDF available in English?

Yes. Under Settings › General › Language you choose English or German. With “Automatic”, GetVaultPDF uses German on a German Windows and English on any other; the change takes effect after a restart. The main window, menus, sidebar, settings and properties panel are in English.

GetVaultPDF is made in Germany, and the translation isn’t finished yet: some dialogs and messages, the AI assistant’s panel and the installer are still in German; their translation will follow in upcoming updates. The AI assistant answers in the language of your question; AI summaries and the title and tag suggestions for Paperless are currently in German.

Do I need an internet connection for the AI?

No. The AI runs via Ollama on your computer or on a PC on your company network. You only need the internet once, to download Ollama and a language model. Depending on your choice, models are about 5 to 19 GB.

What kind of PC do I need for the AI assistant?

16 GB of RAM is recommended. A graphics card with 8 GB of VRAM or more makes the AI much faster. GetVaultPDF shows whether each model will run well on your PC. In the office, a more powerful PC on your network can also run the AI for everyone.

Do I need Microsoft Office?

No. GetVaultPDF opens Excel and CSV files itself, and the Word export works without Office too. GetVaultPDF only needs Microsoft Office or the free LibreOffice to open Word and PowerPoint files.

Can I use GetVaultPDF without AI?

Yes. Ollama is optional. Scanning, text recognition, editing, forms and all other features work without AI.

What data is sent when I activate the license?

Only your license key and a random device name, once, to Lemon Squeezy. Documents, file names or contents are never transmitted. After that, GetVaultPDF checks the license on your PC only.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Viewing, searching, printing, the AI assistant and sending to Paperless remain available. Editing, saving and exporting work again as soon as you activate a license. Your files stay unchanged either way.

Are updates included?

Yes, all version 1 updates are included in the price. Download the new version from this page and install it over the old one. Your license and settings are kept.

Does GetVaultPDF work with Paperless-ngx?

Yes. “Send to Paperless” transfers the document to your own Paperless-ngx server, via its API or through the consume folder. The AI can suggest a title and tags (currently in German). Your access token is stored encrypted in your Windows user account.

Windows shows a SmartScreen warning during installation. Is that normal?

Windows warns about new programs from small publishers that don’t have many downloads yet. To start the installation anyway:

  1. Click “More info” in the warning window.
  2. Check that the file is shown as “GetVaultPDF_Setup.exe”.
  3. Click “Run anyway”.
