Privacy policy

As of October 25, 2026

This is a courtesy translation. Only the German version (Datenschutzerklärung) is legally binding.

At a glance

This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking and loads no third-party content such as fonts, maps or videos. Personal data is only processed when you visit the site (technically, by the host), contact me, buy GetVaultPDF, activate a license in the app, the app checks for updates or it downloads the speech recognition model once.

The homepage contains a small script of its own. In your browser, it checks whether your language setting matches the page and, if not, shows a link to the other language version. It also pauses the animations while they are out of view. Nothing is stored and nothing is transmitted.

Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; for companies certified under it, there is an adequacy decision by the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR).

More information: GitHub’s privacy statement

Contact by email or phone

If you contact me, I process the information you provide (such as your name, email address, phone number and your request) to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request concerns a purchase or support, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise. I delete the data once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Privacy in the app

GetVaultPDF processes your documents exclusively on your computer. The app sends no documents, file names or usage data to me or to third parties; documents only leave your computer when you send them yourself (see below).

License activation

During the one-time activation, the app sends your license key and a randomly generated device name to Lemon Squeezy (technically, your IP address as well). After that, the app checks the license locally only. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Update check

From version 1.4, the app asks GitHub at every start whether a new version is available (see “Hosting on GitHub Pages”). Technically, your IP address as well as the program name and version number are transmitted, but no documents, license or usage data. If you decide to install an update, the app downloads the installer from GitHub. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in offering you bug fixes and security updates. You can turn the check off at any time under Settings › General.

AI features and Paperless-ngx

The AI runs on Ollama, a separately installed program – on your computer or, if you set it up, on a server on your local network. GetVaultPDF connects to no other addresses for this, and it refuses Ollama’s cloud models before any document text is sent. If you download a language model through the AI assistant, Ollama fetches it from its model library at ollama.com; no document data is transmitted in the process.

Documents are only transferred to Paperless-ngx when you trigger the transfer yourself, and only to the server you entered yourself.

AI assistant memory

If you use the memory feature, the AI assistant remembers personal details you give it. GetVaultPDF stores them encrypted on your computer and does not transmit them to me or to third parties.

Dictation

Dictation in the AI assistant recognizes speech offline on your computer. Recordings stay in memory; they are neither saved nor transmitted. The app downloads the speech recognition model once, the first time you click the microphone, from Hugging Face (Hugging Face, Inc., USA). Technically, your IP address, the program name and version number, and which model files are downloaded are transmitted – no recordings and no document data. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. If you don’t use dictation, nothing is downloaded.

More information: Hugging Face’s privacy policy

Sending to nearby devices (LocalSend) and by email

While GetVaultPDF is running and receiving is turned on (the default; you can turn it off under Settings › LocalSend), the app is visible on your local network to devices running the LocalSend app, showing your computer’s name and operating system. Files go directly and encrypted from device to device, with no server on the internet and nothing sent to me. If you want to be asked before each transfer, turn off “Accept incoming transfers automatically” there.

“Send by Email” hands the document to your own email program. GetVaultPDF itself sends nothing; the message is only sent from there, by you.
